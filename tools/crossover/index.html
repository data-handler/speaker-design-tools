Speaker Design Tools / Crossover

Crossover

Simulates a passive crossover network for each driver from its measured response (.frd) and, optionally, its measured impedance (.zma), via standard two-port (ABCD) network theory, then sums the drivers into the modelled acoustic total — with a level trim, acoustic offset and polarity switch per driver to line them up, quick presets for standard filter shapes, and an off-axis/estimated power response for each driver and for the system as a whole.

Inspired by the work of the late Jeff Bagby. Not affiliated with or endorsed by his estate.
